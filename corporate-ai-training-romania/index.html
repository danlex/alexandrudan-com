Session 01

Prompt Engineering System

Teams learn the CROTATFC framework — Context, Role, Objective, Tasks, Audience, Tone, Format, Constraints — and build reusable prompt libraries with review standards for consistent outputs.

Deliverable: team prompt playbook with reusable templates.

Session 02

Custom AI Assistants

Participants deploy role-specific assistants in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, configured against real internal documents, company language, and process constraints.

Deliverable: role-based assistants configured for real workflows.

Session 03

AI Design Sprint

Leaders identify and prioritize 3-5 high-impact automation pilots using the AI Opportunity Card and the Score = (Volume × Minutes × Impact × Feasibility) / Risk formula.

Deliverable: prioritized pilot roadmap with owners and KPIs.
