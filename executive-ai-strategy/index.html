In Short

What 1:1 executive AI strategy advisory is.

1:1 executive AI strategy advisory is a confidential, recurring engagement between one senior leader — a CEO, CTO, CIO, or Chief AI Officer — and an experienced AI systems architect. It exists to make the hard AI decisions well: where to invest, what to build versus buy, how to move agents and LLMs into production reliably, and how to stay governable under the EU AI Act. Alexandru DAN delivers it as ongoing 1:1 sessions or as a fractional / interim Chief AI Officer.

Signature Framework

The Executive AI Decision Stack.

The Executive AI Decision Stack is a seven-layer framework for the order in which a C-suite leader should make AI decisions. Each layer depends on the ones beneath it: skip a layer and the ones above it wobble. It maps every decision to the executive who should own it — CEO, CTO, CIO, or Chief AI Officer — so accountability is explicit before anything gets built. It is the backbone of every 1:1 advisory engagement below.

1 · Owner: CEO / Board

Mandate

Who owns AI, what risk is acceptable, and what "winning" means. Skip it and every downstream AI decision is contested and nothing ships.

2 · Owner: CEO + CAIO

Portfolio

Which problems get AI, and in what order — scored by Volume × Minutes × Impact × Feasibility ÷ Risk. Skip it and pilots chase novelty instead of value.

3 · Owner: CTO + CIO

Sourcing

Build, buy, or partner — decided per initiative, not by default. Skip it and you get duplicated spend and lock-in nobody chose.

4 · Owner: CTO

Architecture standards

What every AI system must meet to run in production — orchestration, reliability, and the agent stack. Skip it and demos never harden into systems.

5 · Owner: CTO + CAIO

Evaluation gates

What a system must pass before it scales — evaluation suites, drift checks, reliability thresholds. Skip it and silent quality regressions reach customers.

6 · Owner: CAIO + CIO

Governance

How you stay accountable and EU AI Act-ready — human oversight, documentation, and risk controls by design. Skip it and compliance becomes a fire drill.

7 · Owner: CAIO

Operating model

How these decisions recur as the organization scales — org design, talent, cadence, and budget cycle. Skip it and AI stays a project, never a capability.

How It Works

Decisions first, decks last.

Method

From decision map to governed roadmap

  • Map the decisions: surface the real questions on the table — investment, build-vs-buy, architecture, org, risk — instead of restating generic AI trends.
  • Score the portfolio: prioritize AI initiatives with the AI Design Sprint scoring model, Score = (Volume × Minutes × Impact × Feasibility) / Risk, so capital follows evidence.
  • Set the standards: evaluation, reliability, and human-oversight gates that keep agentic and LLM systems safe to scale.
  • Govern by design: EU AI Act-aligned controls, documentation, and accountability built into the roadmap, not bolted on after. Implementation guidance, not legal advice.

FAQ

Common questions about 1:1 executive AI advisory.

Who is a 1:1 AI strategy advisor for CEOs and other executives?

Alexandru DAN provides confidential one-on-one AI strategy advisory to individual executives — CEOs, CTOs, CIOs, and Chief AI Officers. He is an Applied AI Systems Architect, CEO at TVL Tech, and Visiting Associate AI Professor at ASE Bucharest, combining board-level strategy with hands-on production-AI and governance experience.

What does a Chief AI Officer (CAIO) advisory engagement cover?

The AI operating model and org design, the AI investment portfolio and roadmap, build-vs-buy decisions, agentic and production-AI architecture standards, evaluation and reliability gates, and EU AI Act-aligned governance — delivered as advisory to an existing CAIO or as a fractional / interim CAIO who stands up the function.

What is a fractional or interim Chief AI Officer?

A fractional Chief AI Officer is a senior AI leader engaged part-time to own AI strategy, governance, and delivery standards without a full-time hire. An interim CAIO does the same for a fixed transition period — for example while you build the function or recruit a permanent leader. I provide both, anchored in production-AI and EU AI Act experience.

How is 1:1 executive advisory different from general AI consulting?

General AI consulting works with teams to build systems. The 1:1 advisory is a confidential thinking partnership for a single executive: pressure-testing decisions, reviewing roadmap and vendor choices, preparing board narratives, and translating between business, engineering, and governance. It complements — and often precedes — hands-on AI consulting delivery.
