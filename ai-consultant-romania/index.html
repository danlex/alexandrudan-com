How It Works

A scoring-first methodology, not a slideware exercise.

Method

From opportunity map to production system

  • AI Design Sprint: identify and score 3-5 automation candidates using the AI Opportunity Card and the Score = (Volume × Minutes × Impact × Feasibility) / Risk formula.
  • Evaluation first: define scenario suites and quality baselines before scaling any pilot, so regressions are caught by design.
  • Architecture second: orchestration, memory, retrieval, and observability mapped to the team that has to run the system.
  • Governance always on: policy, risk review, and accountability embedded directly into roadmap and release processes.

Working principle

Build less demo AI, ship more production AI

Companies that delay AI capability building survive only by luck. The engagement model reflects that: concrete artifacts, measurable pilots, and systems your teams can operate without me.
