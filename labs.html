FAQ

About these Labs projects.

What are the Alexandru DAN Labs?

A space for experimental work in generative AI and interactive web: Suno Lab for generative music, AutoResearch for autonomous research workflows, Particles for a GPU-computed WebGL gallery, and Treasure Hunt for an interactive quest experience.

Are the Labs projects live?

Yes. Each project links to a live site — Suno Lab, AutoResearch, Particles, and Treasure Hunt are all publicly accessible.

How do the Labs relate to the workshops?

They are the applied playground behind the technical AI keynotes and workshops — the same production and evaluation discipline, explored in public experiments.
