Technical Proof of Work

How real AI systems were designed, tested, and stabilized.

Case Study 01

ClassBuddy style workflow AI in production

  • Context: assistant workflows needed orchestration, traceability, and controlled execution.
  • Problem: unreliable agent behavior under changing prompts and user variability.
  • Architecture: event-driven workflow graph with explicit human checkpoints.
  • Stack: FastAPI, Inngest, LangGraph, Gemini, Tavily, PostgreSQL, Redis.
  • Improvement: better operational predictability and faster issue isolation in production.

Case Study 02

Conversational AI testing and regression discipline

  • Context: chatbot behavior changed after model and prompt updates.
  • Problem: silent quality regressions across intents and edge-case conversations.
  • Architecture: repeatable scenario suites with baseline comparisons and drift checks.
  • Stack: scripted test harnesses, evaluation scorecards, and human review loops.
  • Improvement: higher release confidence and fewer production regressions.

Case Study 03

Data Spaces plus AI, trust and sovereignty

  • Context: ecosystem projects required interoperable and compliant AI collaboration.
  • Problem: balancing innovation speed with trust, governance, and policy alignment.
  • Architecture: data-space aligned design with explicit governance controls.
  • Stack: AI service layers, policy-aware integration patterns, secure data workflows.
  • Improvement: practical adoption frameworks for multi-organization AI initiatives.
